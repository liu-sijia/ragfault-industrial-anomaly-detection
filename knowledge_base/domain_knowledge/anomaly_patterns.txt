SKAB Water Circulation System - Diagnostic Decision Guide

## ANOMALY DETECTION THRESHOLDS

### Z-Score Interpretation Guide
- |z| < 1.0: NORMAL - Value within normal operating range
- 1.0 < |z| < 1.5: MILD DEVIATION - Monitor closely, may indicate early warning
- 1.5 < |z| < 2.0: MODERATE ANOMALY - Likely developing fault, investigate
- 2.0 < |z| < 3.0: SIGNIFICANT ANOMALY - Probable fault, action recommended
- |z| > 3.0: SEVERE ANOMALY - Definite fault, immediate attention required

## FAULT PATTERN DIAGNOSTIC RULES

### Pattern 1: Valve Partial Closure (Rate z < -2 AND Temp z > 1)
- Primary Indicator: Volume Flow RateRMS drops significantly (z < -2)
- Secondary Indicator: Temperature rises slightly (z > 1) due to reduced cooling flow
- Fault Type: Mechanical - Valve malfunction
- Action: Check valve position and actuator

### Pattern 2: Bearing Imbalance / Wear (Accel1 z > 2 OR Accel2 z > 2)
- Primary Indicator: Single or dual accelerometer readings elevated
- Pattern: One sensor higher than the other indicates localized fault
- Fault Type: Mechanical - Bearing degradation
- Action: Schedule bearing inspection

### Pattern 3: Cavitation (Multiple features |z| > 1.5 simultaneously)
- Primary Indicator: Multiple sensors show abnormal readings together
- Pattern: High vibration + Temperature change + Flow instability
- Fault Type: Hydraulic - Cavitation due to low inlet pressure
- Action: Check pump inlet conditions

### Pattern 4: Temperature Extreme (Temp z < -3 OR Temp z > 3)
- Primary Indicator: Temperature severely outside normal range
- Low Temperature: Possible excessive flow or cooling failure
- High Temperature: Possible pump overload or cooling system failure
- Fault Type: Thermal - System overheating or overcooling
- Action: Inspect cooling system immediately

### Pattern 5: Combined Sensor Anomaly (2+ features |z| > 1.5)
- Primary Indicator: At least two features showing significant deviation
- Pattern: Indicates systemic issue affecting multiple components
- Fault Type: Complex - May involve multiple subsystems
- Action: Comprehensive system diagnostics recommended

## SENSOR RELATIONSHIP RULES

### Normal Operation Signatures
- All sensors within |z| < 1.5
- Accelerometer1RMS and Accelerometer2RMS roughly balanced (ratio 0.5-2.0)
- Temperature stable with minor fluctuations
- Flow rate consistent with setpoint

### Abnormal Signatures
- Accelerometer Ratio > 3.0 OR < 0.3: Indicates bearing or mechanical issue
- Rate-Temp inverse correlation broken: May indicate valve or flow control problem
- Sudden multi-sensor shift: Indicates external disturbance or fault onset

## DECISION MAKING GUIDELINES

### When to Classify as ANOMALY (YES)
1. ANY single feature with |z| > 3.0
2. TWO OR MORE features with |z| > 1.5 simultaneously
3. ANY accelerometer reading with z > 2.0 (high vibration)
4. Valve pattern: Rate z < -2.0 AND Temp z > 1.0
5. Accelerometer ratio abnormal: > 3.0 OR < 0.3

### When to Classify as NORMAL (NO)
1. ALL features have |z| < 1.5
2. Accelerometer ratio between 0.5 and 2.0
3. Temperature within normal operating range

### Safety Margin Rule
- In borderline cases (1.3 < |z| < 1.7), lean toward ANOMALY
- False alarms are preferable to missed detections in industrial safety

## REFERENCE: SENSOR NORMAL RANGES
- Accelerometer1RMS: 0.02-0.05 g (normal vibration)
- Accelerometer2RMS: 0.02-0.05 g (normal vibration)
- Temperature: 75-95°C (normal operating range)
- Volume Flow RateRMS: 30-150 L/min (normal flow range)

## COMMON FAULT COMBINATIONS

### High Flow + Low Temperature = Cooling System Issue
### Low Flow + High Temperature = Valve Restriction or Pump Problem
### High Vibration + Normal Flow = Bearing or Mechanical Fault
### All Sensors Abnormal = Systemic Problem or Sensor Fault
