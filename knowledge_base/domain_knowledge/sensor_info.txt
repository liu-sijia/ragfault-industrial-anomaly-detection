SKAB Water Circulation System Sensor Information

1. Accelerometer1RMS (Accelerometer 1 RMS)
   - Location: Bearing housing 1
   - Measurement: Vibration acceleration RMS (Root Mean Square)
   - Normal Range: 0.02-0.05 g
   - Purpose: Monitor mechanical vibration of rotating equipment (pumps, motors)

2. Accelerometer2RMS (Accelerometer 2 RMS)
   - Location: Bearing housing 2
   - Measurement: Vibration acceleration RMS
   - Normal Range: 0.02-0.05 g
   - Purpose: Monitor vibration of the opposite bearing

3. Temperature
   - Location: Pipeline outlet
   - Measurement: Water temperature (Celsius)
   - Normal Range: 75-95°C
   - Purpose: Monitor system thermal balance, detect overheating or cooling anomalies

4. Volume Flow RateRMS (Volume Flow Rate RMS)
   - Location: Flow meter
   - Measurement: Fluid volume flow rate RMS
   - Normal Range: 30-150 L/min
   - Purpose: Monitor water flow rate, reflect valve opening and pump performance
